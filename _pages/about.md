Hello! I will join the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.eecs.psu.edu/departments/EECS-Departments-Computer-Science-Engineering3.aspx) at The Pennsylvania State University as a Ph.D. student. Previously, I was a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC), supervised by [Dr. Lu Cheng](https://lcheng.org/). My research focuses on conformal prediction, causal inference, large language models (LLMs), memory management, reflection, and multi-modal LLM-as-a-Forecaster.

Before that, I obtained my B.S. degree from Zhejiang University of Finance & Economics and my M.S. degree from Worcester Polytechnic Institute, where I was supervised by [Dr. Kyumin Lee](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~kmlee/).
